ScanBoard is an Android keyboard with a scanner built in. Point, scan, and the result pastes straight into your text field.

Download on Google Play

What it does

Built-in scanning

QR codes and common barcodes scan right from the keyboard. No switching apps, no manual copy-paste.

Full keyboard

A Gboard-style QWERTY layout with symbols, so ScanBoard works as your everyday keyboard too.

Smart paste

Optionally send Enter, Next, or Tab right after a scan for fast form filling and data entry.

Material You

Dynamic color theming on Android 12+ makes the keyboard match your phone's look.

How it works

  1. Install ScanBoard and enable it in Input Method Settings.
  2. Select ScanBoard from the keyboard picker.
  3. Tap Scan code, point at a code, done — it's pasted.
