NEVER LEAVE YOUR APP

ScanBoard is a keyboard first. It doesn't open an app, and it never takes over your screen. It types.

Point it at a label and the code lands in the field your cursor is already sitting in — your WMS, browser-based inventory, carrier portal, shipping manifest, email, chat, spreadsheet, or a remote desktop session on the server. You keep working the screen you were already on, and nothing gets copied or pasted on the way in.

  • Any text field, any app — desktop, web, or a bare terminal session. No WMS integration, no API, no plugin.
  • No app switching, no clipboard, no retyping SKUs, lot codes, or tracking numbers.
  • Scan history holds your recent codes, so a mis-drop never means walking the aisle again.

SLIDE TO ZOOM. REACH FARTHER.

Most scanning keyboards give you one fixed read window. You bring the code to them.

ScanBoard has a zoom slider instead. Slide it and the scan area widens, so a label at arm's length and a label at the far end of the pallet both read. Reach a code on the top of a stacked load, across an aisle, or on the far side of a carton you haven't picked up yet — without walking to it, without setting the box down, without slowing your pick rate to satisfy the scanner.

It's the one feature the other scanning keyboards don't have.
